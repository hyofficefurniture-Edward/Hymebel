Full-Wall Custom Wardrobes in Almaty & Astana: 2026 Guide

Hymebel Editorial Team · Hongye Furniture Group · 2026-09-30 · Updated 2026-10-02 · 7 min

A full-wall custom wardrobe is a floor-to-ceiling storage system engineered to the exact dimensions of your room. In Almaty and Astana, demand for built-in wall wardrobes in new residential projects keeps growing. Pricing is usually quoted per projection area, meaning width multiplied by height.

Key takeaways
  • Custom wardrobe pricing is based mainly on projection area (width × height)
  • Quality rests on three pillars: panel material, hardware quality and professional measuring
  • Moisture-resistant MDF, plywood and natural wood veneer are the most stable options
  • EAC TR CU 025/2011 certification is mandatory for entering the Kazakhstan market
  • Production takes 30-45 days; rail freight offers a reliable route into Kazakhstan

How Pricing Works: What Projection Area Really Means

The cost of a custom wardrobe is calculated primarily by projection area: the width of the unit multiplied by its floor-to-ceiling height. This method lets a homeowner estimate a realistic budget before detailed drawings exist. The base price covers carcass panels, door systems and standard interior fittings. Drawers, mirrors, LED lighting and glass partitions are typically quoted as add-ons.

According to 2026 industry research, interest in full-wall storage systems in the premium residential segments of Almaty and Astana continues to grow steadily. It is therefore smart to request quotes from several manufacturers based on the same projected area. Always ask what the quoted price includes in terms of panel material, hardware and internal fittings. Otherwise, an apparently low figure can become expensive once the full specification is added.

To obtain an accurate quote, prepare your room dimensions, ceiling height and the position of sockets and vents in advance. The more precise your measurements, the more reliable the estimate. A trustworthy manufacturer will always be ready to share technical drawings and material specifications. If a supplier refuses to document the scope from the very first stage, treat that as a warning sign.

What Should You Check Before Signing? Panels, Hardware and Measuring

Panel material is the foundation of a wardrobe's lifespan. For the dry, heated interiors of Kazakh winters, moisture-resistant MDF and plywood are the most dimensionally stable choices. Always request compliance with E1 or E0 formaldehyde emission classes. A carcass made of cheap chipboard tends to sag after a few years and misaligns the doors.

Hardware is the invisible quality you feel every single day. Soft-close hinges, slide systems rated for heavy loads and metal fittings should meet European standards. Each door opens dozens of times a day, so cheap hardware fails first. Ask for hardware brands and warranty terms to be listed separately in the quote.

Measuring service is the decisive stage of any project. Perfectly flat walls are rare: wall deviations, floor level differences and hidden utilities must all be accounted for. Professional measuring ensures every panel fits to the millimetre. Ordering without accurate measurement almost always means restarting production and delivery.

Quiet Luxury: Natural Wood Veneer and Earth Tones

The newest premium interiors in Kazakhstan increasingly favour a calm, balanced aesthetic over flashy shine. At the heart of this direction lies natural wood veneer: the genuine grain of oak, walnut and ash. Matte finishes reveal the timber's authentic texture and reduce light reflection. Such surfaces age gracefully instead of dating the room.

Earth tones provide the harmony that works with heated walls and stone floors. Sand, clay, smoky grey and warm beige shades echo the natural light typical of Almaty and Astana apartments. Contrasting black profiles or invisible handles add a restrained accent to the composition. The result is an interior that stays relevant for many years.

In this context a full-wall wardrobe is not merely storage but the compositional centre of the room. Continuous veneer makes the whole wall read as a single panel, while recessed profiles let door joints almost disappear. It is exactly this level of execution that defines how a bedroom or living room is perceived. Quality materials and disciplined craftsmanship justify every part of the investment. For residential and villa interiors, our villa furniture range is built on exactly this quiet-luxury principle.

From Factory to Almaty: Lead Times and EAC Compliance

The typical production cycle for a custom wardrobe runs 30-45 days from approved drawings. This window covers panel cutting, veneer pressing, lacquering and hardware fitting. When planning an order, add the freight leg to your calendar, knowing that rail transport carries most volumes. As a result, the full cycle from order to installation can be scheduled precisely in advance.

Entry into Kazakhstan requires furniture to comply with the EAC TR CU 025/2011 technical regulation. This document confirms the safety of materials and the limits of formaldehyde emissions. An experienced export manufacturer ships EAC documents together with the cargo. That prevents customs delays and keeps the project schedule intact.

The rail corridor runs via Khorgos and the Kazakh trunk line, offering a predictable, stable route into Central Asia. For smaller urgent orders, TIR trucking through Khorgos is the faster alternative. Discuss the logistics scheme with your manufacturer as part of the quotation. This way no hidden costs remain in the budget.

Frequently asked questions

How is a full-wall wardrobe priced in Kazakhstan?

The base price is calculated per projection area: wardrobe width multiplied by floor-to-ceiling height. Drawers, mirrors, lighting and special internal fittings are quoted as add-ons. An exact quote requires your real dimensions and the selected materials.

How long do production and delivery take?

Production takes 30-45 days. Rail delivery to Central Asia runs 9-10 days, while TIR trucking via Khorgos takes 5-7 days. The manufacturer compiles the full schedule once the order is confirmed.

Can I order without on-site measurement?

Yes, you can submit a room plan and wall dimensions, and the factory will prepare technical drawings from them. Still, professional measuring is recommended to account for wall deviations and utilities. Without precise measurements, the risk of misfitting panels rises sharply.

What certifications are required and which do you hold?

Entry into Kazakhstan requires EAC TR CU 025/2011, which our products meet. Hongye Furniture Group also holds eight international certifications. All documents are shipped together with the cargo.
