Marble Dining Tables in Tashkent: 8-10 Seater Buying Guide

Hymebel Editorial Team · Hongye Furniture Group · 2026-09-30 · Updated 2026-10-02 · 7 min

An 8-10 seater marble dining table is the centrepiece of large family gatherings, typically 240-300 cm in length. In Tashkent such tables are becoming the heart of the home for weddings, holidays and weekly family dinners. The key decisions are comparing natural marble with sintered stone and matching the size to your room.

Key takeaways
  • For 8-10 seats, a table length of 240-300 cm is recommended
  • Leave at least 90-100 cm around the table for chairs and walkways
  • Natural marble offers unique veining and cool elegance; sintered stone offers stain resistance and easy care
  • The culture of large family gatherings in Uzbekistan shapes demand for oversized dining tables
  • Rail delivery to Tashkent takes 9-10 days; production runs 30-45 days

Natural Marble or Sintered Stone: The Real Comparison?

Natural marble is a stone whose every slab is one of a kind. Its veining and tonal shifts cannot be reproduced by any factory, so your table is a single, unrepeatable piece. Marble is famous for its cool, noble surface that instantly raises the perceived level of a space. At the same time it demands care with acids: citrus juice, vinegar and some cleaners can etch the surface.

Sintered stone is an engineered material fired at extremely high pressure and temperature. It is virtually non-porous, so coffee, wine and oil do not stain it. It shrugs off hot plates and knife marks and needs almost no special care. For a large family where the table works hard every day, sintered stone can be the practical choice.

Both materials can be the right answer, because the question is your lifestyle. If the table serves ceremonial gatherings and restaurant-level hosting, natural marble defines the moment. If the table faces children, endless tea and plov every day, sintered stone keeps its temper. According to 2026 industry research, demand in Central Asia is growing for both materials. For homes where the dining table anchors daily family life, our villa furniture range is designed around exactly that.

How to Match Table Size to Your Room

For an 8-10 seater, a length between 240 and 300 cm is the most balanced solution. A 240 cm top seats eight comfortably, while 280-300 cm gives ten guests room to breathe. Width should fall between 100 and 120 cm so a central runner plus plates on both sides fit. A table of this scale is not just furniture; it is where a large family gathers.

Keep at least 90-100 cm of free space around the table so guests can stand up and pass without pushing chairs back. If the walkway leads to another room, 120 cm is smarter. When measuring the room, account for door swings and paths between other furniture. In Tashkent homes the kitchen and living space often merge into one room, so the table defines the zoning line.

When planning, sketch the room and place a cut-out of the table's exact footprint on it. Draw the chairs, including head seats, which claim another 30-40 cm of depth. Also check clearances between a pendant lamp and the tabletop height. If dimensions are uncertain, send the room plan to the manufacturer and ask for engineering support.

What the Culture of Big Family Gatherings Demands from a Table

In Uzbekistan the dastarkhan is an expression of generosity on a grand scale. Weddings, family milestones, annual holidays and iftar dinners bring dozens of guests to a single table. Families in Tashkent therefore treat a large, ceremonial dining table as the centrepiece investment of the home. The table itself is a statement of respect for guests and of family standing.

Such gatherings place unusual demands on the structure. Heavy platters, plov cauldrons and long tea evenings add real load. Base rigidity, the design of leg junctions and the thickness of the stone top are therefore core technical questions. A table with wide supports and a slab of 2 cm or thicker will serve for decades.

According to 2026 industry research, the floor area of new homes in Uzbekistan keeps growing, and dining spaces designed for family gatherings are multiplying. This is exactly what explains the demand for 8-10 seater tables. Natural marble veining and deep earth tones suit ceremonial tables best. Such a composition expresses the hospitality of a large family in a single glance.

Care and Longevity: Marble vs Sintered Stone in Daily Life

Natural marble asks for a few simple rules in daily care. Wipe acidic spills immediately with a dry cloth so stains never sink in. Cleaning with a neutral pH soap solution is sufficient. One professional refinishing per year restores the stone's original depth.

Sintered stone is deliberately engineered like a training ground. Stains, discolouration and heat marks are practically foreign to it. Its non-porous surface gives microbes nowhere to settle. For families who treat the table as a real workstation, this translates into around-the-clock reliability.

In both cases, thickness and edge treatment define lifespan. A reinforced substrate beneath the stone slab distributes load evenly. Rounded edges are safer for children and guests alike. Whatever material you choose, ask the manufacturer about these technical details explicitly.

Frequently asked questions

What size should a dining table for 8-10 people be?

A length of 240-300 cm and width of 100-120 cm is recommended. A 240 cm top seats eight, while 280-300 cm seats ten comfortably. Leave at least 90-100 cm of clearance around the table.

What should I do if wine or lemon juice spills on marble?

Wipe it up immediately with a soft dry cloth, since acid etches the stone. If a mark remains, arrange professional polishing. A pre-applied hydrophobic treatment significantly reduces the impact of spills.

How long does delivery to Tashkent take?

Production takes 30-45 days, and rail delivery to Tashkent runs 9-10 days. EAC documents travel with the cargo, which reduces customs delays. The full schedule is provided once the order is confirmed.

Can a large table fit a compact room?

With 90-100 cm of clearance, a 240 cm table fits a room of roughly 20 m². If space is tighter, floor-to-ceiling custom storage elsewhere in the room can free up the area needed. Send us the room plan and our engineers will calculate the right configuration.
